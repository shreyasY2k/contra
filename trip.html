Practical Notes

// before you leave
  • Documents: vehicle RC, insurance, valid PUC certificate, and all drivers' licenses — the route crosses state lines four times.
  • Rough budget: fuel + tolls for a sedan across the ~2,800 km loop typically runs ₹16,000–22,000. Get an exact toll estimate from an app like Tollguru closer to departure.
  • Driving discipline: no night driving through ghat sections (Hampi–Dandeli, Dandeli–Goa, Goa–Pune via Amboli/Kolhapur). Start long-drive days by 6–7am. Rotate drivers hardest on Day 6 and Day 11 — the two 9–10hr days.
  • Cave closures checked: Ellora (closed Tuesdays) and Ajanta (closed Mondays) have both been checked against the group's actual dates and confirmed clear on Day 9 and Day 10.
  • Day 1 departure is load-bearing: leave by 5–5:30am or Hampi sightseeing gets rushed.
